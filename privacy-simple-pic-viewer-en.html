Simple Photo Gallery Privacy Policy

1. Personal Information We Collect

This app does not directly collect any personal information from users.

2. Device Permissions

This app requests the following device permissions:

  • Storage read permission: Used to display photos and videos stored on your device.
  • Storage write permission: Used for photo rotate/flip editing features.
  • Internet permission: Used for displaying ads and the WiFi transfer feature.
  • WiFi state permission: Used for the WiFi transfer feature.

Data accessed through these permissions is processed only on your device and is not transmitted to any external server.

3. Data Storage

App settings and trash data are stored only on your device.

Photo and video files are read directly from your device's storage and are not separately copied or uploaded.

4. Advertising Services

This app uses Google AdMob advertising.

AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve ads.

For more details, please refer to the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Third-Party Disclosure

This app does not provide users' personal information to third parties.

However, data may be collected by Google through the advertising service (Google AdMob).
