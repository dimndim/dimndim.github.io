Sweepic Privacy Policy

1. Personal Information Collection

This app does not directly collect any personal information from users.

2. Device Permissions

This app requests the following device permissions:

  • Storage Read Permission: Used to scan and display photos stored on your device for duplicate detection.
  • Storage Write Permission: Used for photo deletion, moving, and copying features.
  • Internet Permission: Used to display advertisements.

Data accessed through these permissions is processed only on your device and is not transmitted to external servers.

3. Image Analysis

This app analyzes images to detect duplicate or similar photos.

All image analysis is performed locally on your device. No images or analysis results are uploaded to external servers.

4. Data Storage

App settings are stored only on your device.

Photos are read directly from your device's storage and are not copied or uploaded elsewhere.

5. Advertising Service

This app uses Google AdMob for advertisements.

AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to provide advertisements.

For more information, please refer to Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Third-Party Disclosure

This app does not share your personal information with third parties.

However, data may be collected by Google through the advertising service (Google AdMob).
